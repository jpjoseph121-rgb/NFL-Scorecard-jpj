🏈 How to play

NFL 2026 Pick'em · free · no sign-up

Think you know football better than your friends? Prove it.

Before kickoff, call the final score for both teams. Once the game starts, your pick locks and sits beside the real result — no changing your mind, and no bragging without receipts. Points are calculated automatically from official scores, and a live leaderboard keeps the whole group honest.

Play with friends in a minute

Open the League tab, enter your name and join with the group's invite code. Share the code with up to 25 friends, family, colleagues or fellow fans and everyone lands on the same board. Follow your favorite team's full season in My Team, browse games day by day, check the standings, and turn on a reminder that nudges you 30 minutes before a game locks. Add it to your Home Screen and it works like any other app.

Only your best result on a game counts, so a bold call is always worth it. The leaderboard also tracks exact scores — the real bragging right. Tap any player to see their picks next to the finals once games have kicked off.
